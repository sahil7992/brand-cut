## Digest: what the skill does because of this research
- **Flash safety (section 5):** WCAG 2.2 SC 2.3.1 general flash = a pair of opposing changes in relative luminance >= 0.10 where the darker state < 0.80; red flash = saturated red R/(R+G+B) >= 0.8 transitions (2.2: UCS distance > 0.2; 2.0 draft: (R-G-B)x320 change > 20); fail when more than 3 in any 1 s over > 25% of a 10 degree field (341x256 px at 1024x768). Broadcast (Ofcom / ITU-R BT.1702): 20 cd/m2 change, darker < 160 cd/m2, 25% of screen. `qa/flashcheck.py` implements the strict union plus the house flood rule (one flood per 8 s) and passes 15/15 calibration cases.
- **Platforms (section 4):** 9:16 safe rect x 70-940, y 270-1240 (union of Meta Reels 14%/35%, TikTok 220-240/480-660/right 140, Shorts 225/575); 4:5 1080x1350 keeps the centre 1012 px for Instagram's 3:4 grid; 1:1 hero in the centre 810 px; 16:9 10% title safe. Master -14 LUFS integrated, -1.0 dBTP (no platform publishes an official target except YouTube's attenuate-only behaviour). YouTube 2160p60 SDR 53-68 Mbps; Reels/TikTok 1080x1920 60 fps. Encoded in `formats/presets.json`.
- **ElevenLabs (section 2):** speed (0.7-1.2) works on Multilingual v2 and Flash/Turbo v2.5, not on v4 (and v3 ignored it locally); audio tags only on v3/v4 and they get read aloud on v2-family models; /with-timestamps returns character alignment; Starter output max mp3_44100_128; Multilingual v2 = 1 credit/char, Flash/Turbo 0.5-1; SFX ~100-200 credits per generation; Eleven Music on Starter: 900 credits/min, 17 min/month cap, commercial online use (no film/TV/radio), no artist names in prompts. ## Research rules adopted, and the three we override
Adopted into `SKILL.md` and `templates/SPEC.md`: rules 1-12 and 14-22 of section 1's "Rules we will enforce" (flash, easing direction, durations, spring limits, focal count, holds, sentence case, reading limits, minimum type size, banned tells, glass only over real layering, real UI, SFX per action, ducking, native formats, verification gates).
Overridden, with reason:
- Rule 13 "no text animated per character, no motion blur on type": the per-word blur-in and per-letter tracking collapse are the measured grammar of the strongest references (Glow, Wise, Pulse); we keep per-word blur-in and limit per-character work to typing inside real UI.
- Rule 14 "decorative marquees" and "pulsing dots": our marquee rows carry real lead messages (content, not decoration), and dots only morph into pills, never pulse on beats.
- Rule 6 "no single move over 1.0 s": the slow continuous camera drift over a whole scene is allowed (it is a drift, not a move).
