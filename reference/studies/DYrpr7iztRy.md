## Summary

A 21 s brand film that is really one sentence performed in four acts on a single living background. Act 1 (0-5.5 s): "Creativity is like a game," is typed word by word with a light-blue karaoke highlight; the words peel away and are replaced by tiny tool glyphs (pen, move cross, cursor, T, eraser, magnifier, pin, home) that shuffle in slots around the word "like" and then condense into "and yes, we like to play." Act 2 (6.5-12.6 s): the sentence collapses to "to play" and its full stop becomes the bullet of a 3D drum/list wheel of services (Brand Identity, Brand Strategy, Brand Story, Graphic Design, Video Production, Video Editing, Content Strategy, Platform Branding, Growth Marketing) that clicks on the beat then spins fast. Act 3 (12.6-16.3 s): a 3D card carousel of real portfolio work (logos, B&W portraits, product shots, a heat-map card, a bar-chart card) rotates behind a centre focus card. Act 4 (16.3-20.97 s): three dots (outlined, ring-dot, solid) orbit, the background cuts to black for one second, the dots settle into the SDS mark and a shape-wipe reveals the "SDS" wordmark plus "SANDYDESIGNSTUDIO". The blue silk-light background never changes during Acts 1-3, which glues everything together.

## Per shot detail

It is a slow-moving gradient mesh (looks like a displaced light render, not a flat CSS gradient): the bright band travels slowly about 5 % of width over 20 s (visible as a changing position at 0.5 s, 8.5 s, 13 s). Vignette in the corners. This one plate is the reason the film feels expensive and consistent.

**Shot 1: typed headline.** Text bbox measured at 1.9 s: x 21.4-75.4 % (54 % of frame width), y 46.5-55.2 % (8.7 % of frame height including descenders), centred at 47 % x, 50.6 % y. ## Why this does not look AI-generated

1. One continuous silk-light background plate across 17 s; no scene changes of look.
2. Real client footage and real logos on the cards (specific, odd: Tamil emblem, Hawaii roofing, B&W street photo); AI cards would be generic gradients.
3. The wheel has real optical rules: opacity, scale and skew are all driven by distance from one focus line; focus row exactly at 50 % height, bullet on the left; rows wrap.
4. Step timing: holds 0.4-0.5 s then 0.1 s expo snaps, then an accelerando into a spin; humans choose a rhythm, generators loop.
5. Identity-preserving morphs: full stop becomes the bullet; three dots become the logo mark.
6. Typography restraint: one neo-grotesk, sentence case, one size, two colours; only the logo is heavy.
7. Micro-detail: karaoke tint on the current word, 2-frame viewfinder brackets, icons with uniform stroke, tiny all-caps tagline only inside the lockup.
8. A deliberate black beat before the lockup so the brand mark lands on a changed background.
9. Cards contain their own animation at different tempos (typing, heat map, bar chart) which reads like screen-recorded work, not stock.

## What would make it look AI

1. Glossy random 3D objects in the carousel instead of real work.
2. A wheel whose rows only fade (no scale, skew or perspective), or whose rows are evenly bright.
3. Text that morphs letter by letter through garbage glyphs (the glyph shuffle works because the icons are crisp vector tool icons).
4. Constant easing (all ease-in-out 0.8 s) with no holds or acceleration.
5. A background that looks like a purple/blue AI gradient with noise and sparkle particles.
6. A logo reveal that dissolves from noise instead of a shape wipe.
