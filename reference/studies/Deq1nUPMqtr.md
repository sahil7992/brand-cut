## Summary
A 44.6 s SaaS launch film for an AI marketing agent that opens by talking to the viewer ("You built something. A business. An idea. Worth knowing about. Now.") using fluted-gradient glass type on white, turns "Now" into a gradient orb orbited by city chips, introduces the product with "Meet holo", types a domain into a glass composer and launches a 3D mascot on a jetpack, then demonstrates the product in four swapping UI cards (Ads, Socials, Emails, SEO), flips an "Autopilot" toggle on while a ring of generated ads bursts out, pulls back from a monitor that goes from "All Day" to "All Night", scrolls the agent's task list (a drum), shows "Generating" thumbnails and a floating field of ads, then closes on a phone on a desk, "Chief Marketing Officer" with the mascot inline, a live revenue counter "45 708$" over gradient bars and the URL "tryholo.ai". The narrative: second person hook (0-8) - brand reveal (8-15) - product proof as a four-item list (15-22) - autopilot promise (22-30) - scale and proof by volume (30-37) - role claim and number (37-42) - CTA URL (42-44.6).

## Why this does not look AI-generated
1. Real, legible UI in every card (a hot-sauce ad with a headline, an Instagram profile with counts, an inbox with sender names, a Google SERP, a payout screen) rather than blurred placeholder UI.
2. Typed text has irregular human cadence (gaps 0.06, 0.14, 0.06, 0.07, 0.13, 0.07 s) and a blinking caret.
3. Letters are drawn on (stem grows from the bottom, arc sweeps) with a long dwell before the speed-up, and the glass rim has a physical, tube-like highlight; not a fade.
4. The fluted-glass blob is built from discrete columns with staggered offsets (a tactile glass effect), not a smooth gradient.
5. Depth order is controlled: icons in front of the headline, hand behind, the composer bar in front of the mascot's arm.
6. The 3D scenes carry correct lighting continuity (day to night lighting moves along the pegboard shadows, speaker highlights).
7. Colour discipline: white page and one gradient family (orange, pink, violet, periwinkle) used for brand moments only; photographs carry the saturated product colours.
8. Quiet sound holds before reveals (14.5-15.0, 24.75-25.0) and per-event SFX, not a constant bed.
9. Camera language differs scene to scene (push-in, pull-back, macro, dolly) but each stays slow; quick moves (5 frame pull-back) are masked by blur.
10. Brand-specific content (hot sauce named Hotline) is consistent across 12 scenes: one hypothetical customer, one palette of generated work.

## What would make it look AI
1. Gradient letters with a smooth neon outer glow and no physical rim or dwell, appearing by fade.
2. UI cards with garbled microcopy or generic "Lorem" bars.
3. A mascot that morphs (changing eyes or limb count) between frames instead of one consistent 3D render.
4. Photos with no theme (random subjects) in the ring of cards instead of five nameable categories with chips.
5. Every scene change as a 0.5 s crossfade; Holo uses hard cuts at beat positions plus object-led morphs.
6. Typing that appears at perfectly even 4-frame intervals.
