## Summary
A 14 s personal-brand logo sting. A dotted ring and a white star bloom out of black, collapse into a hand-painted hyperspace tunnel (blue and yellow streaks), a hand-drawn lightning zigzag crosses the centre, and the tunnel blows out into one cream flood (0.45 s). A single tilted "m" with a blue/yellow offset shadow lands on cream, a hard cut to black follows, and the word "matheus" builds letter by letter with doodles (yellow diamond, blue leaf, sparkles). The "a" lives in a yellow paper-cut square that wobbles, spins to a diamond, turns into a blue blob, scribbles into a yellow spiral, then disappears and pops back. A yellow zigzag slash wipes the word out and a four-point sparkle closes the film. It is the opposite of a template: everything is drawn frame by frame on twos with grain, and the sound design carries 60 percent of the effect.

## Flash profile
- 1 flood only: up 5.08-5.26 (+0.117, +0.17, +0.531 mean luminance over 3 frames; changed-pixel fraction 0.31, 0.54, 0.70), peak mean L 0.944, down at 5.71 (-0.934, 99 percent of pixels). Flood duration about 0.45 s, size full frame.
- Opposing transition pairs in any 1 s window: 1 (only the up/down at 5.1-5.7). Max flashes per second: 1 (under the 3 per second limit), but the down edge is a one-frame black-to-cream slam that is hostile; our rule: soften it (ramp out >= 6 frames or cap L at 0.6).
- The rest of the film is under 0.03 mean L (nearly all black), so no other hazard.

## Copy structure
There is no copy, only the wordmark "matheus" (lowercase). Structure: hook = ring and star bloom (0-2.7), tension = tunnel acceleration and zigzag (2.7-5), payoff = flood and "m" (5-5.7), proof = the word builds with personality (5.7-12.7), CTA = the clean wordmark held 12.7-13 and the slash close.

## Why this does not look AI-generated
1. It is animated on twos/fours: 50 percent duplicate frames in the tunnel (0-4.8 s), 29-32 percent in the wordmark. AI motion is smooth on ones.
2. Grain and dither halos replace blurred gaussian glow (the hot core has a stipple edge).
3. The black is #080509 and the "white" is #F0F0F0 / cream #FEFAED: no pure #000/#FFF anywhere.
4. Typography has an inked rough edge and the "a" is a separate character with its own life: letterforms are art-directed, not set.
5. Colour discipline: exactly blue #3028E0, yellow #F8C000, white, black; every doodle uses only those.
6. Doodles are asymmetric and imperfect (lightning polyline has jittery vertices, spiral is not a true Archimedean).
7. Anticipation in sound: the boom is 0.25 s before the flood so the picture feels hit, plus silence 9-11 s makes the later pops land.
8. Scale contrast: the star core is 4 percent of the frame, then 200 px radius blowout, then a tiny "m" (about 7 percent of frame width) on a giant empty cream: scale jump as a hook.

## What would make it look AI
1. A smooth gaussian bloom with soft purple/blue gradient instead of grain-edged shards.
2. Perfectly vector, uniform radial lines with identical length and constant speed.
3. A flood that eases in AND out over 1 s on a 30 percent pulse, repeated on every beat.
4. Letters appearing with a uniform 4-frame blur-in stagger, perfectly centred, no doodles.
5. A generic sans in pure #FFFFFF on #000000 with a rainbow gradient.
