## Summary
A 15 s "motion reel" in nine numbered chapters (01 TIMING, 02 TYPE, 03 FORM, 04 DEPTH, 05 SYSTEMS, 06 FLOW, 07 INTERFACE, 08 RHYTHM, 09 FIN) drawn as a Bauhaus/Swiss-brutalist poster system: four flat colours (black, orange, cream, blue plus pink and yellow accents), giant heavy grotesque type, and one recurring red-orange dot that is the hero of every transition. A ball bounces with real squash and stretch on a hand-drawn ground line, balloons into the orange card "MAKE IT MOVE.", the full stop becomes the next scene (zoom-through), shapes morph with Figma-style selection boxes and riso mis-registration, a cube splits into a Rubik's cube and flies through the camera, a halftone ring pulses, neon tubes draw on, a toggle is clicked, a dashboard counts to 100 percent, a 1.3 s montage of seven icons, and the dot lands as the full stop in "Motion Reel '26." with the footer "Design - Animation - Direction / 15 sec / 900 frames / 60 fps / Available for new projects". Every scene is a different motion discipline demonstrated, not described.

- 4.38-4.42: cream to blue, -0.174, -0.212, -0.118 (3 frames, -0.5 total). Flood down.
- 10.50: +0.156 (71 percent). 11.07: -0.30 (98 percent). 11.25: -0.104. 11.82: +0.649 (99 percent).
- 3 flood events in 1.4 s (2.95, 4.38, and none other until 10.5): up/down pair 2.95-4.38 (1.43 s apart, below 3 per second). Max count of such transitions in any 1 s window: 3 (10.5, 11.07, 11.25), forming at most 2 opposing pairs per second. This is the limit of our rule (3 per second) and it only works because the colours are saturated (mean L 0.1-0.8) and the changes are scene swaps, not white strobes.
- Full-frame lum floods about 1 per 3.5 s on average in the first half and 1 per 1.3 s in the montage, so our rule (one flood per 8 s) is stricter than this reference.

## Why this does not look AI-generated
1. Physics is correct: gravity-constant fall, squash only on the contact frame, restitution 0.68, stretch only while moving. AI bounces are floaty.
2. A single recurring object (the dot) is the through-line and every transition is a shape swap (match cut), not a crossfade.
3. Strict palette: 4 flats plus 2 accents, no gradients except the cube shading; exact hexes reused in all nine chapters.
4. Documented craft: HUD, chapter counter, timecode, footer, Figma selection boxes and labels: designers see these as authentic "process" cues.
5. Motion blur is selective and directional (horizontal on slides, radial on zooms, vertical on counters) and follows the move, not a global filter.
6. Risograph offset layers (blue and yellow copies lagging 2-3 frames) give each morph depth with zero 3D.
7. Typography is real: heavy extended grotesque at 34 percent frame height with tight tracking, plus a contrast serif for the year, and the dot is the actual full stop.
8. Timing varies: ball 1.5 s, type 1.5 s, shapes 0.1 s each, icons 0.1-0.2 s: rhythm of long hold, burst, hold. Not one fixed beat length.
9. Sound events land on exact frames (toggle click on the click frame).

## What would make it look AI
1. Morphing shapes done as a diffusion style warp rather than clean vector paths.
2. A cube with fake reflections and random texture, no consistent face colours.
3. Type that glitches or has inconsistent letterforms (typical of generated text).
4. Gradient backgrounds with a neon glow behind every object instead of flat colour fields.
5. Beat-synced scale pumps on every kick (this reel does not).
6. Identical blur length and identical 8-frame easing on every transition.
