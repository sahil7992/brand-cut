# SFX library

Names resolve through INDEX.json (mix.py `sound`). `align: onset` puts the first audible hit on the cue time, `align: peak` puts the loudest moment on it. Times in seconds. Licences: ElevenLabs-made = ElevenLabs Starter commercial terms.

Only files present in this folder are listed. More SFX can be pulled from Pixabay or Freesound (their licences let you use the files in a film but not redistribute them, so keep them out of the repo).
